# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: March 5, 2025_

This Privacy Policy describes how Tracer ("Application"), developed and maintained by marini-pietro, collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using or accessing the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal personal information, and only if you choose to provide it. The information we collect includes:

### 1.1. Email Address
If you choose to submit a bug report via email, you may provide your email address along with your email password. This information is used only for the purpose of sending bug reports and is never stored or shared beyond this use. Your email password is not saved and is used only to send the email report.

### 1.2. Google Login Information
If you choose to log in using your Google account, we use Google’s OAuth 2.0 authentication system. This allows you to log in to the Application without manually entering your email password. We do not store or access your Google credentials; authentication is handled securely by Google.

### 1.3. Non-Personal Data
We do not collect any other personal information beyond what you provide voluntarily. We may collect non-personal data such as device type, application usage, and interaction statistics for the purpose of improving the Application's functionality. However, this information cannot be used to personally identify you.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

- **Bug Reports**: To send email responses when you submit a bug report.
- **Authentication**: To log you in via Google OAuth 2.0.
- **Improvement of Services**: We may use aggregated non-personal data to improve the performance and features of the Application.

We will not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others.

## 3. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Application may integrate with third-party services such as Google for authentication. These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

- **Google OAuth 2.0**: If you log in using your Google account, Google’s privacy policy governs the information exchanged. We do not store or use your Google credentials.

The Application uses icons and visual assets sourced from the internet, which are licensed for use in accordance with their respective terms. Credit and licensing information for these assets can be found in the README file of the project repository. These assets do not contain any personal information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be effective immediately upon posting. We encourage you to periodically review this page for any updates.

You have the right to:

- **Access**: You can request to see the information we have collected about you.
- **Delete**: You can request the deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
